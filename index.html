NeuroAdapt Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

NeuroAdapt is an educational research prototype exploring associations between wearable physiological measurements and voluntary mood ratings. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or predict medical or mental-health conditions.

Data accessed

With the WHOOP account holder's authorization, NeuroAdapt is intended to access recovery information (including heart rate variability and resting heart rate), sleep duration and timing, and daily cycle information (including strain and average heart rate). A participant may separately choose to record mood ratings.

Purpose

The data will be used only to explore personal trends, build experimental baselines, and test research methods. WHOOP data from periods when a different person wore the device will be excluded from the study dataset where those periods can be identified.

Storage and access

Mood ratings and timestamps entered through the NeuroAdapt website are stored in a private Supabase cloud database hosted in Canada, protected by account authentication and row-level access controls. WHOOP measurements may be stored in the same private database once integration is enabled. WHOOP separately maintains its own records under its own privacy terms. GitHub Pages hosts the public interface but does not contain private health records.

Sharing

The project does not intend to sell or publicly share health measurements. Access to research records is restricted to authorized signed-in accounts. Supabase processes stored records to provide the database service. No health measurements, API tokens, or account credentials should be committed to a public GitHub repository.

Retention, deletion and withdrawal

Research records will be retained only as needed for the prototype. Signed-in participants can delete their mood entries from the app; other records can be removed by the project administrator. The WHOOP account holder can revoke the application's authorization. Deleting NeuroAdapt records does not remove records retained by WHOOP.

Changes

This policy will be updated before the project changes how it collects, processes, stores or shares information. Collection should not begin until the actual application follows these practices.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the NeuroAdapt project administrator through the contact address provided in the WHOOP developer application.
