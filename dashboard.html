Experimental mood prediction

A simple on-device machine-learning experiment uses previous-day WHOOP readings to estimate your mood on the following day. It does not diagnose, assess, or predict depression or any mental health condition.

Waiting for data.

Requires at least 20 days with both a mood rating and WHOOP measurements from the preceding day. We test predictions on later days that were not used to train them and compare them with a simple average-mood baseline. The model only becomes available if it beats that baseline. Predictions are uncertain and should not be treated as facts about how you feel.
